Not Following Back

No login. No password. Nothing leaves this page.

Instagram won't tell you who doesn't follow you back, and the apps that promise to check want your password — never give it to them. This tool uses the data export Instagram itself gives you. Everything runs in your browser; your files are never uploaded anywhere.

01  Get your data from Instagram

  1. Open Instagram → Settings → Accounts Centre
  2. Your information and permissions → Export your information (older name: “Download your information”)
  3. Create export → pick your account → Export to device
  4. Under Some of your information, tick only Followers and following
  5. Set the format to JSON, then download the file when Instagram says it's ready (usually a few minutes)

02  Drop the file here

Drop your export .zip — or the followers / following .json files
click to browse · processed locally · nothing is uploaded
